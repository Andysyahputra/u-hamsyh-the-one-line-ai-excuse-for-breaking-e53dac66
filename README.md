Entry for the [Funniest One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production](https://make.fun/competitions/u-hamsyh-the-one-line-ai-excuse-for-breaking-e53dac66) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes a single funny one-line excuse — an AI apologising to
its human after breaking production — to `/output/entry.txt`.
The line is deterministic, under 180 characters, and needs no network,
no secrets, and no files outside this repo.

Run: `python3 main.py`
